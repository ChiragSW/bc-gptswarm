\section{Limitations}

The planned reproduction cannot guarantee access to the original 2024 model snapshots. Hosted-model nondeterminism may remain after seeding local libraries. A reduced MMLU subset may have limited power, while broader GPTSwarm claims about tool use and node optimization will not be reproduced initially. API cost may restrict seeds and graph sizes.

The proposed contribution is deliberately narrow because closely related work already studies efficient routing, pruning, and model substitution. An expected-token constraint does not impose a per-query hard cap and may be satisfied on average while individual executions exceed $B$. Token count also imperfectly represents latency, energy, or monetary cost. Discrete graphs, binary rewards, and stochastic model outputs can destabilize primal--dual learning, and an infeasible budget can drive the dual variable upward without producing a valid solution. We mitigate this with a measured feasibility floor, normalized violation, validity constraints, and complete trajectory logging. These restrictions will be declared before experiments, and no unrun result will be inferred from prior work.
