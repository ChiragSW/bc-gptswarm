\section{Limitations}

The planned reproduction cannot guarantee access to the original 2024 model snapshots. Hosted-model nondeterminism may remain after seeding local libraries. A reduced MMLU subset may have limited power, while broader GPTSwarm claims about tool use and node optimization will not be reproduced initially. API cost may restrict seeds and graph sizes. These restrictions will be declared before experiments, and no unrun result will be inferred from the original paper.
