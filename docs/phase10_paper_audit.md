# Phase 10 Paper Audit

Audit date: 2026-09-18

## Scope

This audit closes the manuscript-writing phase. It checks claim-to-evidence alignment, implementation consistency, bibliography provenance, generated artifacts, compilation, and page layout. It does not create the Phase 11 reproducibility package.

## Claim and implementation alignment

- The abstract reports the controlled-study quantities from the generated summaries: original accuracy-only GPTSwarm at 0.250 accuracy and 251.1 tokens/question; middle-budget BC-GPTSwarm at 0.236 and 236.7; a 5.8% token reduction; and 4.0% normalized budget violation.
- The abstract and conclusion state the negative result directly: the study does not establish an accuracy improvement or tight-budget convergence under the fixed 12-iteration allowance.
- The introduction's three contributions match the repository: a reduced GPTSwarm edge-optimization reproduction, an implemented primal--dual expected-token constraint, and controlled comparisons using accuracy, tokens, violations, and graph structure.
- BC-GPTSwarm is distinguished from GPTSwarm by feasible-policy conditioning, conditional policy gradients, token telemetry, and a projected non-negative dual variable. The manuscript does not claim ownership of GPTSwarm's graph representation, node roles, or baseline edge optimizer.
- Internal development-phase labels and draft placeholders were removed from the reader-facing manuscript.
- Statistical language respects the three independent optimizer seeds, treats the 72 seed--question pairs only as a secondary conditional analysis, and avoids equivalence or superiority claims.
